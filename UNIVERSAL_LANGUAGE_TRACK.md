
## Permanent retirement: named cognitive-faculty candidate branch

## Stone boundary

Stone-specific semantics come from the frozen canonical paper, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22164942`, not from old repository summaries or experimental helper code.

The current canonical structure is entity-relative and referent-relative. `Phi(q,r)` is an orientation in the closed L1 octahedral referent; L1 is development, L2 is experience, and realization revises the referent `r -> r'` and requires a newly warranted orientation. Purpose, meaning, and answerability are structural relations of the entity-referent orientation.

Repository Stone math files are bounded formal/serialization experiments. They cannot become a hidden evaluator inside canonical C, and they do not supply a universal evidence-to-coordinate psychometric rule.

## Context-loss procedure

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `agi/CHECKPOINTS.md`, and any current architecture decision named there.
2. Search implementation, tests, and history before declaring a mechanism absent.
3. Identify the existing source of truth and preserved failure.
4. Classify the change as reuse, connection, extension, or replacement.
5. Prefer reuse/connection. No copied semantic/status/planning authority.
6. Preserve exact reality contact and `unknown != false`.
7. Treat historical removed branches as audit evidence, not permission to reconstruct them.
