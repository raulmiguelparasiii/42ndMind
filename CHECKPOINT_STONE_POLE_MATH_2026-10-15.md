## Source semantics preserved

- Empathy: concern for affected welfare.
- Practicality: real-world constraint on action and consequence.
- Knowledge: informational grounding / grasp of represented reality.
- Wisdom: contextual discernment: interpretation of grounded information through context, limits, proportion, implication, consequence, and application.
- Answerability: materially relevant reality retains governing and corrective authority over orientation.
- Insulation: the existing orientation governs admission/interpretation of reality so correction is subordinated to preservation of the stance.
- Absence / Null: no developed orientation or formed relationship with the declared referent.

These definitions are used as corrigible structured primer contact. They are not hidden branches in `F`.
